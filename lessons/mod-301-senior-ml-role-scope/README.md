# mod-301-senior-ml-role-scope: The Senior ML Engineer Role: Scope, Verbs, and Hand-off Contracts

**Estimated effort:** 10 hours

This module is the orientation for the rest of the track. You are moving from the build-altitude ML practitioner workflow (level 20 — [`ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/ml-engineer-learning)) into senior tech-lead altitude on the ML engineering ladder. Before you spend the next ~200 hours on architecture, evaluation, reliability, and cross-team scope, this module puts the ladder into words: what changes at L30, how to read that change out of a job posting, where your ownership ends and a peer track's begins, and which of the following nine modules you personally need to grow into.

Every chapter below maps to a learning objective; every exercise operationalises one. There is no long-form lab in this module — the exercises produce the artefacts (a verb audit, a hand-off map, a growth plan) that you will re-use in the tech-lead simulation project.

## Learning objectives

- Articulate what a Senior ML Engineer owns end-to-end vs. what a mid-level (L20) owns and what a Staff (L40) owns.
- Read a job posting and identify the seniority-defining verbs (own, lead, mentor, set roadmap, partner with, raise the bar for).
- Map the hand-off contracts to peer specialist and peer platform tracks — where this role ends and another begins.
- Author a self-assessment that names the areas of the following modules the learner most needs to grow into.
